Midi Mode 2 local test 0.0.7 uses Bank 1 as the MIDI track-name template. Renaming in another bank overrides only that bank; its Parts share those names, while unedited tracks continue to follow Bank 1. Explicit blank names are saved as overrides. Existing 0.0.6 saved names and channel profiles import without changing the original checkpoint files. The local updater and listing use the new package; physical testing of the new overrides remains pending.
